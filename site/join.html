Requirements
  • 18+ preferred. We're a mature crowd.
  • A microphone. Highly recommended. We play on Discord voice.
  • Maturity. Goof off, but know when not to.
  • Respect. For members and everyone else.
  • A clean record. No game bans. No cheating or exploiting, ever.
// Rules of engagement

Server rules

01General conduct
  • Be respectful to everyone, in game, in voice and in Discord.
  • Keep it mature. Banter is fine; harassment, hate speech and slurs are not.
  • No cheating, hacking, macros or exploiting bugs. Report bugs instead of using them.
02PvP & raiding
  • Each server's PvP and raiding rules are pinned in its Discord channel.
  • Combat logging to dodge a fight counts as exploiting.
03Bases, safe zones & vehicles
  • Don't build in or block traders, safe zones, roads or loot spawns.
  • No fighting or griefing inside safe zones.
  • Don't leave vehicles blocking garage spots or trader areas for others.
04Staff, reports & appeals
  • Report problems in Discord with the server, the time and details.
  • Staff decisions stand in the moment; appeal calmly in the Ban appeals board afterwards.
  • Don't ask staff for spawns, items or compensation outside the official process.

Staff have final say. Breaking rules can mean a warning, a wipe of your gear or a ban.
