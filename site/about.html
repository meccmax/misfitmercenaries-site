// Service history

How we got here

  1. Late 1990s

    Born in Britannia

    A handful of players band together in Ultima Online. The name, the jokes and most of the people stick.

  2. The MMO years

    A galaxy far, far away — and then some

    Star Wars Galaxies and its emulators, EverQuest II, Fallen Earth, APB, SWTOR, ArcheAge and New World.

  3. Many names

    Aliases on file

    Misfits, Misfit Mercs, The Pimp Legion of Doom, Lumberjack Commandos, FDWS (Future Droids and Weapon Systems) and The Hemp Collectors.

  4. Milsim & survival

    Boots on the ground

    Arma 3 and the original DayZ Mod pull the crew toward squad play, survival and running our own servers.

  5. Today

    Our own servers, our own mods

    Self-hosted DayZ, Arma Reforger, Conan Exiles and Dune Awakening servers, plus in-house mods and open-source tools.

// The community

How MM works

No ranks to grind and no dues to pay. Just a long-running crew, an open door, and servers built around the people who play on them.

Open door

Anyone can join

Our community Discord is open to everyone. Behind it is a core crew of about 40 who have been gaming together for years.

Cross-game

One crew, many games

A night might start in DayZ and end in Arma Reforger, Conan Exiles or Dune Awakening. When we move on to a new game, we move together.

Your voice

Players shape the servers

After every wipe we collect feedback and run polls on the economy, AI, events and gear. The results go straight into the next season.

Staff

Run by players

An admin team keeps the servers, mods and backend running. A staff team handles support, moderation and the community side.

Self-hosted

Our hardware, our rules

Every server runs on hardware owned and paid for by members. No rented boxes, no outside owners, no ads.

No pay-to-win

Funded in the open

Donations are voluntary and buy nothing in game. We publish exactly what the servers cost. See the numbers.
