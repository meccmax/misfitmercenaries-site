// Terms

The fine print

  • Donations are voluntary
  • Donations are considered gifts
  • Donations are non-refundable
  • Donations come with no expectations
  • Donations do not purchase services
  • Donations do not grant benefits

By donating, you acknowledge you are simply helping with server expenses.

// Where it goes

Donations only cover

  • Server hosting
  • Mod licensing and development
  • Backend tools
  • Infrastructure costs
  • Maintenance

Donations help offset costs. They never fund profits.
